SMALL ACTS. BIG DIFFERENCE.

Reunions, made simple.

Whether you’ve lost something or found it, you’re only a few steps from making someone’s day.

01

Spot it

Search the board or check where you last saw your item. A place and a date can go a long way.

02

Share it

Post what you know. Add a photo if it’s safe to share, and see posts that might be a match.

03

Bring it home

Check a private detail only the owner knows, then meet at a staffed campus desk for handover.

LOOK OUT FOR EACH OTHER

A safe return is a happy return.

CampusFind helps people connect; it cannot verify identities or prove ownership. Keep identifying details out of public posts and arrange exchanges at a staffed campus location.

Ask for a private detail before handing an item over.
Meet at a staffed campus desk, not alone.
Blur IDs, addresses, and personal details in photos.
